ABOUT HBR

Hot Box Records (HBR) is an independent underground music collective driven by raw lyricism, versatile flows, and heavy production. Built from the ground up, HBR brings together sharp songwriters, vocalists, and sound engineers dedicated to pushing authentic hip-hop forward.
